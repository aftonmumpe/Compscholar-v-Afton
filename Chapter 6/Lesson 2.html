Paris, France

Known as the City of Light, Paris is famous for its art, fashion, and delicious pastries. You can stroll along the Seine River, visit the Eiffel Tower, or spend hours exploring the world-class museums like the Louvre. It’s a beautiful city full of history and charm.

Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto is a city where ancient traditions meet modern life. With peaceful temples, colorful gardens, and historic tea houses, it’s the perfect place to experience Japan’s cultural heritage. In the spring, the cherry blossoms make the whole city feel like a dream.
